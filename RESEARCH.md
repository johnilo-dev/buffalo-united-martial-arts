# Buffalo United Martial Arts Academy — research brief

Verified September 5, 2026

## Executive summary

Buffalo United Martial Arts Academy (BUMA) is a multi-discipline academy at 359 Ganson Street in Buffalo’s RiverWorks area. Its current official website presents Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Muay Thai, MMA, kids martial arts, Judo, Sambo, boxing, submission wrestling and conditioning. The strongest recurring brand signal is not “hardcore fighting”; it is serious technical instruction within a welcoming, family-oriented community.

The new website therefore positions BUMA as a “fight family”: credible enough for competitors, approachable enough for a first-time adult or parent. The assistant knowledge base prioritizes the official website, identifies uncertainty instead of guessing, and cites the passage used for each answer.

## Research objectives

- Confirm identity, location and contact channels.
- Establish the current program and class-time inventory.
- Identify instructors and supported credentials.
- Understand the academy’s differentiation and public reputation.
- Find contradictions or stale claims that should not enter the website or chat knowledge base.
- Convert reliable facts into a useful, bounded RAG corpus.

## Methodology

The investigation began with broad web discovery, then moved to the current official website and contact page. Claims important to a visitor—programs, times, instructor names, address and contact details—were extracted from those primary pages. Google Business information supplied in the project brief and independent directory/review aggregators were used to compare contact and reputation data. Competition listings were reviewed as supplementary evidence. Conflicts and claims found only in older reviews were excluded or explicitly labeled.

## Key findings

### Identity and location — high confidence

BUMA’s official contact page places the academy at **359 Ganson St, Buffalo, NY**, matching the Google listing supplied in the brief and independent directory results. The location is associated with Buffalo RiverWorks and described by the academy as downtown Buffalo.

### Program breadth — high confidence

The current official site describes BJJ, Muay Thai, MMA, kids classes, Judo and Sambo on its program section. Its FAQ adds submission wrestling, boxing and Kru Fit Cardio. This breadth supports a website organized around “one academy, many paths,” rather than a single BJJ-only offer.

### Published schedule — medium-to-high confidence

The official FAQ publishes the following highlights:

- 6 AM BJJ Monday through Friday; gi Monday/Wednesday/Friday and no-gi Tuesday/Thursday.
- Kids BJJ Monday/Wednesday at 5:30 PM.
- Kids Muay Thai Tuesday/Thursday at 5:30 PM.
- Tuesday/Thursday 7:30 PM no-gi.
- Monday 8 PM submission wrestling.
- Tuesday 8 PM Judo.
- Tuesday/Thursday 4 PM Kru Fit Cardio.
- Saturday 9 AM Muay Thai, 10 AM family/fundamentals and kids BJJ, and 11 AM all-level gi BJJ.
- Sunday 8 AM competition class and noon boxing.

Class schedules are operational data and can change without a page rebuild, so the site labels these as “published class times” and advises confirmation before a first visit.

### Instructor roster — high confidence for names; medium for summarized credentials

The official site names Josh Ketry (BJJ), Khru Keith Forant (Muay Thai and youth), Larry Backlas (BJJ), Anthony Gugino (Judo/grappling), Michael Christopher (boxing) and Jon Kokotajlo (Sambo). The card summaries in the concept are conservative versions of biographies published by the academy.

### Affiliation and community — high confidence

The official footer says BUMA has been a member of Essential BJJ under Professor JT Torres since 2021 and links to the We Defy Foundation. The site’s own copy repeatedly emphasizes an inclusive, family-like environment.

### Reputation — high confidence for the supplied snapshot; medium for long-term totals

The supplied Google listing reports **4.9 from 35 reviews**. Independent aggregators show the same 4.9 rating but sometimes older totals of 29 or 31, which is consistent with snapshots taken at different dates. Repeated review themes include welcoming instructors, family suitability, technical learning, cleanliness, safety and a supportive atmosphere.

### Competitive participation — medium confidence

Supplementary competition records include BUMA-affiliated entrants in ADCC trial and regional tournament listings. This supports the academy’s claim that it serves competitors, but individual entry records do not establish overall team results or current rankings; the website makes no unsupported ranking claim.

## Contradictory and rejected evidence

### Two phone numbers

The current official page visibly displays **(716) 671-7197**, while the same page’s click-to-call target points to **(716) 563-0720**. The supplied Google listing also uses 563-0720. The concept makes this discrepancy transparent, uses the visibly displayed official-site number as the primary CTA, and gives the Maps number as an alternate.

### “Established in 1992” claim rejected

One aggregator says the academy was established in 1992, but the surrounding copy incorrectly discusses theater shows and artists. That record appears contaminated by another venue and was excluded.

### Current free trial not confirmed

An older Google review reproduced by an aggregator mentions a free trial. The current official pages reviewed do not clearly confirm a current free-trial offer. The assistant therefore directs visitors to ask, rather than promising one.

### Pricing unavailable

No reliable current membership prices were found on the official pages. Third-party price ranges were generic estimates and were excluded from the knowledge base.

### Opening hours are not the class schedule

Some directory pages label weekdays “open 24 hours,” while the official page says weekday 6 AM to 8 PM and publishes individual class times outside that range. Directory business hours were not treated as class availability.

## Limitations

- No owner interview or private membership material was available.
- Social posts may contain newer schedule changes that are not reflected in the official FAQ.
- The exact age bands, equipment requirements, waiver process, parking guidance, accessibility details and current membership terms remain unverified.
- Review counts change over time.
- Instructor biographies are academy-authored and were not independently credential-checked line by line.

## Confidence assessment

- Address and academy identity: **High**
- Current program names: **High**
- Published class times: **Medium–High**, because schedules are inherently changeable
- Instructor names: **High**
- Instructor credential summaries: **Medium**
- Family/welcoming positioning: **High**, supported by official copy and repeated review themes
- Pricing, trial and discount policy: **Low / unknown**, intentionally not asserted
